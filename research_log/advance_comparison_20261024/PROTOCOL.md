# Additional comparison protocol — 24 October 2026

Question: what is actually demonstrated beyond the closest source constructions, and how much of the current evidence depends on internal baselines or cohort selection?

Source audit checks the authors' public implementation and primary supplement. Absence of a matching executable circuit is a comparison limitation, not proof of novelty or superiority. Do not compare our CCX counts directly to another source's QFT cycles. No manuscript or old raw result is changed.

New data-only control: compare methods on identical complete condition sets. Use both root/bank2/bank4/exact-LP and the stricter set also including simple profit pruning. Separate original/core/independent/scaled panels and initial-incumbent+1/optimum+1 targets. Exclude conditions resolved classically and all-root-rejected conditions from primary model comparisons, but retain explicit exclusion counts. Capped conditions remain censored; no unknown T is imputed. This is an additional analysis, not a prospectively registered experiment.

For logical CCX, total gates and depth, calculate (C+h)sqrt(T), h in {0,0.25,1,10,100} times the exact-LP predicate's cost. Count per-condition winners on the SAME cohort and report every method's ratio to exact LP. Winner selection uses observed T and is an answer-known diagnostic, not a deployable policy or a new optimization algorithm. No regression or claim of measured speedup is made. Workspace remains a separate resource, never multiplied by sqrt(T).

Criteria: a construction difference, repeatable internal improvement, demonstrated improvement over a predecessor, global historical priority and sufficient journal significance are five different conclusions. The evidence must identify which ones are established. Negative outcomes and missing implementations remain explicit. Independent adversarial review follows computation.
