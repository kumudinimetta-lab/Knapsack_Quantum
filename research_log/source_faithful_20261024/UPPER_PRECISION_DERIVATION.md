# Directed rounding control — derivation before implementation

24 October 2026. Candidate stronger same-function baseline; historical priority UNRESOLVED. This does not claim a new rounding principle or a new quantum algorithm. Existing manuscript and pinned builders remain untouched. The previously verified Vedral–Barenco–Ekert primary source, *Quantum networks for elementary arithmetic operations*, Physical Review A 54,147 (1996), Sections III.1 and III.3, supplies reversible constant arithmetic feasibility (https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.54.147). No variable divider is proposed.

Assumptions: positive integer item weights/profits, nonnegative integer capacity, exact density ordering with deterministic ties, integer threshold tau, first fractional suffix item s, feasible prefix, and no relevant overflow. Let v_s/w_s=a_s/b_s in lowest terms with b_s>0. Let P be total whole-item integer profit, and r the capacity remaining immediately BEFORE s, so 0<=r<w_s. The exact fractional LP is U=P+r*a_s/b_s. For integer tau, if U<tau then tau-U>=1/b_s, since b_s*U is an integer.

Choose q_s=ceil(2^k_s*a_s/b_s), and Uplus=P+r*q_s/2^k_s. If r=0 or the ratio is represented exactly, the error is zero. Otherwise 0<=Uplus-U<r/2^k_s <=(w_s-1)/2^k_s. If 2^k_s>(w_s-1)*b_s, the error is strictly below 1/b_s. Consequently U<tau implies Uplus<tau, and U>=tau implies Uplus>=tau. Thus the integer-threshold reject predicate is identical to exact LP on feasible branches, although Uplus is an upper approximation of its numeric value. Compare (P<<k_s)+r*q_s < (tau<<k_s), avoiding the old margin-aware multiplication by wmax. At all-fit/no-split suffix compare integer P<tau; capacity/invalid depth reject separately.

If b_s is a power of two, choose k_s=log2(b_s), which represents the efficiency exactly and needs no conservative error inequality. In particular b_s=1 permits k_s=0. Otherwise choose k_s=bit_length((w_s-1)*b_s), guaranteeing strict greater-than even at powers of two. These are sufficient instance-data choices, not necessary minima. Item-specific precision is compiled into separate constants and wire shifts; the output threshold register and coherent scan interface remain the same as previous controls. Resource effects must be tested rather than assumed favorable.

Width: let S=sum(v_i), tb=bit_length(S+1) (at least 1). For each item, cover (S<<k_s)+W*q_s and ((2^tb-1)<<k_s), then take a shared maximum plus one padding bit. This covers every live comparison on feasible branches. On infeasible branches residual arithmetic may wrap, but the active flag is zero and irrelevant comparisons must not influence the output. Retain compute/copy/uncompute and zero workspace checks.

Counterexample attempt to relaxed precision: w=3,v=1,r=2,tau=1. At k=0, U=2/3 while Uplus=2, so exact LP rejects and upward-rounded LP retains. At k=1 or k=2, Uplus=1 still retains. The chosen sufficient k=3 gives Uplus=3/4<1. Therefore upward rounding alone preserves safety but does not preserve the exact pruning predicate without the error/gap condition. Equality must never be pruned. If a ratio is exactly representable, imposing the conservative inequality is unnecessary, as the b=1 control demonstrates.

Tests required after independent algebra review: arbitrary feasible residual r at every item and thresholds adjacent to U; dyadic and non-dyadic ratios; strict equality; exact order preserved; capacity zero/all-fit/empty/overweight; invalid depth; ignored suffix bits; both target bits; cleanup. First run exhaustive tiny scalar cases, then explicit-gate propagation against independent exact rational reference. Larger benchmark checks remain scoped samples, not dense exhaustive simulation of thousands of wires. No central novelty claim is approved in this derivation.
